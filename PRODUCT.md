# CadVerify

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Manufacturing, design, cost engineering and sourcing teams deciding whether a part can be made, on which equipment, with which materials, and with what resources. These audiences and capabilities are supported by PLATFORM-DNA.md and the existing product routes.

## Product Purpose
Turn a CAD part and its operating requirements into a traceable manufacturing decision. Verification comes before cost; a resource estimate is not a supplier quote or a certification.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The existing Next.js application provides authenticated upload, verification, machine inventory, records, catalog, triage, cost analysis and sharing. Preserve the authenticated workflow and server-side authorization. Public sample exploration must work without an account or a backend. The sample uses the repository's recorded example, with assumptions and process-specific findings visible; changing a sample view does not run a new analysis.

## Brand Commitments
CadVerify is the existing name. The user requests a full website redesign following the buyer-first sequence in the supplied EliseAI case study. Lead with the work the visitor needs handled, then the relevant workflow, evidence, fit and human handoff. The user explicitly chose: try a sample part, then upload their own.

## Evidence on Hand
PLATFORM-DNA.md; existing marketing fixture object.stl: 21.16 mm diameter, 21.43 mm height, MJF PP reference cost $14.14 at quantity 10, drivers 6.39 / 3.89 / 3.82 / 0.04, assumption-based range $8.49–19.80, zero measured outcomes, injection-molding draft finding. Examples are recorded data, never live engine output. No verified customer testimonials or customer outcome statistics are available. Do not invent them. Existing pilot contact: pilots@cadverify.com.

## Product Principles
- Start with the user's manufacturing decision.
- Show what was measured, what was assumed, and what still needs a person.
- Make the sample useful before requesting registration.
- Preserve source attribution, limitations and human review.

## Open Decisions
No production deployment is requested. Backend service availability and credentials must be verified separately from public sample functionality.
