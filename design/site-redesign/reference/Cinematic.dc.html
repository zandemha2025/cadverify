Questions.

Is this a quote?

No. It’s an estimate from your part and your shop’s numbers, with the math showing. Your engineer still signs off.

What files can I upload?

STEP, STL or IGES. Using SolidWorks, CATIA, NX or Creo? Save a copy as STEP.

What if I don’t have all the details?

ScaleCAD fills gaps with standard guesses and labels each one. Missing information is never treated as a yes.

Can it check thousands of parts?

Yes. Upload a zip of up to 10,000 parts and each one is sorted: make it, buy it, needs new equipment, can’t be made, or needs more info. Part of a pilot or Enterprise plan.

Can it run on our own servers?

Yes, as part of a pilot. We agree your security rules before you share anything sensitive.
