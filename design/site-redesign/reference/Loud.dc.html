Questions.
Straight answers.

Is this a quote?

No. It’s an estimate built from your part and your shop’s numbers, with the math showing. Use it to decide and to negotiate. Your engineer still signs off before anything gets made.

What files work?

STEP, STL and IGES. Using SolidWorks, CATIA, NX or Creo? Save a copy as STEP first.

What if I don’t know everything about the part?

Nobody does. ScaleCAD fills the gaps with standard guesses, labels every one, and never turns a missing detail into a yes.

What counts as a check?

One part, one full answer: can it be made, what’s wrong with it, how long it takes and what it costs.

Can it run on our own servers?

Yes, as part of a pilot. We sort out your security and data rules with you before you share anything sensitive.

ScaleCAD gives estimates, not quotes, and it doesn’t sign off parts. Your engineer does that. We just give them the afternoon back.
